FAQ

Common Questions

What do I get for $1,500? +

A 5–6 page website for your business — typically pages like Home, Services, About, and Contact — with a mobile-friendly layout, contact forms, SEO basics, and analytics, live on your domain. If you already take bookings online, your booking system stays connected. Before any work starts, we agree in writing on exactly which pages and features are included, and that's the price.

Can you redesign the website I already have? +

Yes. If you're already paying for a platform like Wix, I can redesign your site on it, so you keep your plan, your domain, and your online booking. If a different setup would serve you better, I'll explain why on the discovery call — and you decide.

Will I own my website or app? +

Yes — completely. For websites, the site runs on your domain and your own hosting or platform account, and any custom code is transferred to you. For iOS apps, you sign up for your own Apple Developer account ($99/year) before development begins, and the app is published under your name from day one. App Store revenue flows directly to your bank account. All custom code becomes your property upon final payment.

Do you offer support after launch? +

Yes. The website care plan is $100/month and covers up to 1 hour of updates — new hours, services, photos, and other small changes — with extra time at $100/hour. Hours don't roll over. Apps and larger sites use monthly blocks at the same $100/hour rate (typically 2 or 5 hours) for bug fixes, content updates, iOS compatibility patches, and App Store resubmissions.

Do I have to sign up for the care plan? +

No, it's optional. Without it, any changes you ask for later are billed at $100/hour, only when you need them.

What happens if my project goes over the estimate? +

Website packages are flat-priced, so the price only changes if you ask for more than the agreed pages and features — and that goes through a written change order first. For hourly projects, I check in with you before approaching the upper end of the estimate, and new requirements go through a written change order before any extra hours are billed. You will never get a surprise invoice.

How do payments work? +

25% deposit upfront against the flat price or the estimated hours, applied to your final invoice. Hourly projects are then billed weekly with itemized hour logs so you can see exactly what was worked on. Payment via Stripe or direct invoice. All work is governed by a written client agreement covering scope, IP transfer, and payment terms.

How long does an iOS app take? +

It depends entirely on scope, which is exactly why we start with a free discovery call. After our conversation I'll provide a written estimate with an hour range. Calendar time also depends on App Store review, which can take 1–7 days per submission. Simple, single-purpose utility apps tend to be the sweet spot.
